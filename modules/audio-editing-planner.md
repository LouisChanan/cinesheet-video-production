# Audio & Editing Planner — Module 07

Design four channels independently: `dialogue`, `ambience`, `sfx`, `bgm`. In general keep dialogue intelligible and location sound credible; not every scene needs all four.

- Dialogue: exact lines, language, mic quality, speaker turn and mouth action; avoid unnatural overlap.
- Ambience: matching environment/room tone, consistent transitions.
- SFX: sync to visible action (spray, wipe, pan sizzling, cat meow), sparing Foley rather than a wall of sound.
- BGM: describe style, energy and timing without embedding copyrighted music; keep beneath speech.
- Editing: recommended cut points, on-screen text as an overlay at editing stage (safer for AI-generated product text), shot/beat markers, intentional lead-in/out, platform safe zone.
- For a family/pet ad, don't infer chemical safety from a cat simply being in the shot.

Deliver to `scenes[].audio`, `scenes[].on_screen_text` and `delivery`.
